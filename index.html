Neychor Pallicurry

Neychor and Beef Pallicurry are a legendary food combination from the Malabar region of Kerala, India, specifically famous in the Kasaragod district. They are typically served together at weddings, festivals (like Eid), and religious gatherings.

Chicken Kheema

Experience the rich and aromatic flavors of our Chicken Kheema - a traditional Indian cuisine made with minced chicken cooked to perfection with fragrant spices, onions, and fresh herbs. This comforting dish captures the essence of authentic Kerala cuisine, offering a perfect balance of spice and flavor that warms the soul. Served with appam, chapati, or rice, it's a beloved favorite that brings families together for memorable meals.
